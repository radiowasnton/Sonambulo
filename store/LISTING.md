La noche, más viva que nunca, con Sonámbulo. Elige modo oscuro o modo claro para cada sitio web según lo desees. Dale a tus ojos solo lo mejor.

• Por sitio: Oscuro, OLED, Filtrado y Normal, más Alto contraste. Cada elección se guarda para cada sitio, y desde el engranaje de cada uno puedes editar toda su configuración.

• Sigue el tema del sistema, tiene horario diario y atajos de teclado.

• 51 idiomas.

## English

**Summary** — the `extDescription` of the manifest:

> Night, more alive than ever. Choose dark or light mode for every website and give your eyes only the best.

**Detailed description**

Night, more alive than ever, with Sonámbulo. Choose dark or light mode for every website, exactly as you like. Give your eyes only the best.

• Per website: Dark, OLED, Filtered and Normal, plus High contrast. Every choice is saved for each website, and its gear lets you edit the whole configuration.
• Dark, with two engines you can pick per website: Styles (recolors the page's CSS and never touches images or content) and Render (an inversion filter that works on almost anything). Pure OLED black or a softer gray.
• Filtered: keeps the original colors and softens brightness, contrast, saturation and blue light.
• High contrast: Increased, Grayscale, Inverted color, Inverted grayscale, and Yellow, Cyan or Green on black, with an intensity slider.
• Remembers every website (and its subdomains) and applies changes instantly, with no reload.
• 170 per-site fixes included, and a Solid background option for pages with background images.
• Follows the system theme, with a daily schedule and keyboard shortcuts.
• Backup and restore of your websites.
• 51 languages.

Privacy: Sonámbulo collects no data and keeps everything in your browser. It only downloads the page's own stylesheets in order to recolor them. Free software under the Apache License 2.0, created by radiowasnton.
